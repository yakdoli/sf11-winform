IsExpandedInitialValue
Gets or sets whether groups should be shown expanded initially. IsExpandedInitialValue will be assigned to groups when the table is categorized, e.g. when GroupedColumns were changed. This value must be set before the GroupedColumns are changed.
RepaintCaptionWhenItemsChanged
Indicates whether the caption row should be repainted when a record that belongs to the group was changed. The setting only has an effect when InvalidateAllWhenListChanged = false.
ShowAddNewRecordAfterDetails
If true, AddNewRecord shown at bottom of group.
ShowAddNewRecordBeforeDetails
If true, AddNewRecord shown at top of group.
ShowCaption
Indicates whether the Caption row is visible.
ShowCaptionPlusMinus
Indicates whether there is a PlusMinus cell next to the Caption.
ShowCaptionSummaryCells
Indicates whether the group caption should display summaries in columns instead of only one large caption bar. The CaptionSummaryRow then lets you specify a SummaryRow with Summary column information.
ShowColumnHeaders
Indicates whether the column headers are visible.
ShowEmptyGroups
Indicates whether a preview is visible when group is collapsed.
ShowFilterBar
Indicates whether the FilterBar are visible.
ShowGroupFooter
Indicates whether a footer is visible.
ShowGroupHeader
Indicates whether a header is visible.
ShowGroupIndentAsCoveredRange
Indicates whether to treat all indent cells for the group as a single covered cell.
ShowGroupPreview
Indicates whether a preview is visible when the group is collapsed.
ShowGroupSummaryWhenCollapsed
Indicates whether summary items are visible when the group is collapsed.
ShowStackedHeaders
Indicates whether the stacked headers are visible.
ShowSummaries
Indicates whether summaries are visible.
SummaryRowPlacement
