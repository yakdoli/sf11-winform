## Slice data {#slice-data style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

The following example describes the slicing of data. .

Date: (FY2002, FY2003, FY2004, FY2005)

Region: (Australia, Canada, France, Germany, United Kingdom, United States)\
Product: (Product A, Product B, Product C, Product D)\
- Volume of sales for Product A. (Shows a table with all regions and dates of Product A sales)\
- Volume of sales in Region United States. (Shows a table with all products and dates in United States Region)
