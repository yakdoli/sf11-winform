## Clock {#clock style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

The Clock control is used to select the time easily just by dragging the hour and minute hands in the clock control.

 

Features

[·      ]Hand dragging support.

[·      ]Extenssive designer Support in both Expression Blend and Visual Studio 2008.

[·      ]Properties to completely customize the control, without the necessity of custom templates.

[·      ]Digital watch can be displayed to cross check the time selected.

[·      ]Built-in visual styles and themes support.
