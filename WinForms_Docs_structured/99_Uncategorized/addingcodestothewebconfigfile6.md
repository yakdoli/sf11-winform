### Adding Codes to the Web.config File {#adding-codes-to-the-web.config-file style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

On the **Solution Explorer**, double-click **Web.config file**. (There will be two web.config files. Refer to the one in the root folder.)


[{border="0"}]Note: The Web.config page appears.[]


{border="0"}

Figure 13: Web.config Page
