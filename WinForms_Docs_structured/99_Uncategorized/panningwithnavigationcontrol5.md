### Panning with Navigation Control {#panning-with-navigation-control style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

Maps can be panned with Pan buttons (TopPan button, RightPan button, BottomPan button and LeftPan button). If you click on a particular Pan button the Map will be panned on the respective directions.
