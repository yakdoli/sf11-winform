### Structure of the Split-Button Control {#structure-of-the-split-button-control style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

The Split-Button control allows you to add drop-down items to button. It also allows the contents to be represented by an image, text, or an image with text.

{border="0"}

Figure 241: Split-Button in OFF state

 

[·      ]Drop-down Button - It is the right most portion of the Split-Button.

[·      ]Drop-down Items - When you click the drop-down button, drop-down items will be displayed.

[·      ]Normal Button - It is the left portion of the Split-Button.

[·      ]Text - Displays the text on the Split-Button.

[·      ]Image - Displays the image or icon on the Split-Button.
