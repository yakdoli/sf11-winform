## Time Mode {#time-mode style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

 

Essential Schedule supports two types of Time Modes.

[·      ]12 Hours - Displays appointments and timeline headers in 12 hours format.

[·      ]24 Hours - Displays appointments and timeline headers in 24 hours format.
