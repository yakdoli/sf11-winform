### Zoom with Navigation Control {#zoom-with-navigation-control style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

With Navigation control the Maps can be Zoomed. When you click on the ZoomIn button the Map will be zoomed in. If click on the ZoomOut button the Map will zoomed out.
