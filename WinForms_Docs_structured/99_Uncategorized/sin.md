### SIN {#sin style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

 

Returns the sine of the given angle.

 

**Syntax**

 

**SIN(number)**

 

where:

**number** is the angle in radians for which you want the sine.

 

[]{#p188}SINH\
\

Returns the hyperbolic sine of a number.

 

**Syntax**

 

**SINH(number)**

 

where:

**number** is any real number.
