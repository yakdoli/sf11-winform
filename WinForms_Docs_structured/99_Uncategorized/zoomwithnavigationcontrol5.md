### Zoom with Navigation Control {#zoom-with-navigation-control style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

 

With Navigation control, the Map can be zoomed. When clicking the ZoomIn button, the Map will zoomed-in. When clicking the ZoomOut button, the Map will be zoomed out.
