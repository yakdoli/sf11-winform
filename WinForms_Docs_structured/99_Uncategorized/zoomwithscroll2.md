#### Zoom with Scroll {#zoom-with-scroll style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

 

The Map gets zoomed, when mouse-scrolling on the map. When scrolled-up, the Map will be zoomed in and when scrolled-down, the Map will be zoomed out.
