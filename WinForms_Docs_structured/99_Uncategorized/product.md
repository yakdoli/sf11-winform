### PRODUCT {#product style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

Multiplies all the numbers given as arguments and returns the product.

 

**Syntax**

 

**PRODUCT(number1, number2, \...)**

 

where:

**number1, number2, \... **are numbers that you want to multiply.
