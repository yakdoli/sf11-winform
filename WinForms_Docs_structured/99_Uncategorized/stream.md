Stream with Rect and Encoder

You can also save to a stream with type of encoder and rectangle portion to be clipped.

 

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **[\[C#]**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
