##### Customizing page effect {#customizing-page-effect style="TEXT-JUSTIFY: inter-ideograph; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; TEXT-INDENT: -36pt; MARGIN: 10pt 0pt 0pt 36pt; tab-stops: 36.0pt"}
