## Assembly Management {#assembly-management style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

Assembly Management consists of Assembly Manager and Multi-Target Manager. 

 

The  utility installs Syncfusion Essential Studio assemblies in the GAC and in the Assemblies folders, provided Visual Studio.NET is installed on the development machine.


{border="0"}Note: The Assembly Manager does not require Visual Studio.NET to be installed.
