### jQueryUIDatePicker {#jqueryuidatepicker style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

 

The jQueryUIDatePicker is a highly configurable plug-in that enables the datepicker functionality in your pages. You can customize the date format, restrict the selectable date ranges, and add buttons and other navigation options easily.
