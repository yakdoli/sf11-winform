##### How to custom paint the XPTaskBar background to have a gradient look and feel {#how-to-custom-paint-the-xptaskbar-background-to-have-a-gradient-look-and-feel style="MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; tab-stops: 18.0pt"}

[] 

The XPTaskBar\'s **Paint** event can be handled to custom draw the background using the LinearGradientBrush as given below.

[]
