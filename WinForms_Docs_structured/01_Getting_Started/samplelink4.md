### Sample Link {#sample-link style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

 

To access a Exporting sample:

1.   Open the Syncfusion Dashboard.

2.   Click Business Intelligence.

3.   Click the **Silverlight** drop-down list, and select **Explore Samples**.

4.   Navigate to Syncfusion.PivotAnalysis.Silverlight.Samples -\> Syncfusion.PivotAnalysis.Silverlight.Samples -\> Samples -\> ExportingDemo.
