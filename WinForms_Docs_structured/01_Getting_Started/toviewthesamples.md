### To View the Samples {#to-view-the-samples style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

1.   Open the **Dashboard**. The **Essential Studio Enterprise Edition** window is displayed.

2.   Select the **ASP.NET MVC** platform from the left side of the window. 

3.   Click the **Run Samples** link. The **Essential Studio MVC Edition** sample browser is displayed.

4.   Select **Grid**.

5.   Select **Context Menu** samples from the **User Interaction** tab and browse through the samples.
