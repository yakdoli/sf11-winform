###### 3.14.7.9.2.1        To enable scroll support for AutoHidden items {#to-enable-scroll-support-for-autohidden-items style="tab-stops: 0pt"}

The Scroll support helps to provide a clear view of the AutoHidden items without having to mouse over and search the items.
