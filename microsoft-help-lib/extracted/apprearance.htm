Apprearance

 

The Skins property allows you to set predefined skins for the control, in order to enhance its appearance.

 

The available skins are as follows:

·      Office 2007 Blue

·      Office 2007 Black

·      Office 2007 Silver

·      Vista

·      Almond

·      Blend

·      Blueberry

·      Marble

·      Midnight

·      Monochorome

·      Olive

·      Sandune

·      Turquoise

·      VS2010
