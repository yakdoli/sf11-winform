Client-Side Events

 

WaitingPopup control raises the client side events to call the client side handlers. To invoke the client side events, follow the below steps.

Setting OnBeforePopup and OnPopup properties to the corresponding client side functions OnBefore and InProgress triggers the appropriate events before the WaitingPopup control is loaded and after the control pops up respectively.

 

Setting OnClosePopup and OnTimeOut properties to the client side functions OnClose and OnFinish, triggers the appropriate events after WaitingPopup is closed and on time out respectively.

 

3.   Build and run the application, and click the links to invoke the functions.
