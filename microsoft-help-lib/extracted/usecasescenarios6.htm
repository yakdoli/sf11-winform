Use Case Scenarios

·      The Toolbar control enables you to overcome the limitation of the application bar which can hold only four items.

·      It also enables you to overcome the space limitation in a mobile application.

·      For some applications that require several items to perform operations such as a painting application, all the items cannot be docked or placed where required due to the phone’s screen size. The Toolbar control enables the items to dock and change position as the orientation changes.
