Prompt Char

 

MaskPosition will be represented by the PromptChar property.

 

XAML

 

<syncfusion:MaskedTextBox Name="maskedTextBox1" Height="25" Width="150"

                          Mask="00/00/0000" PromptChar="_"/>

 

C#

 

maskedTextBox1.PromptChar = "_";
