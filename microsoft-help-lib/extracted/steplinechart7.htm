Step Line chart

Step Line charts use horizontal and vertical lines to connect data points resulting in a step like progression.

Step Line chart can be created through two ways:

·      Builder

·      ChartModel

 

Chart Details

 

Number of Y values per point

1

Number of Series         

One or more

Cannot be Combined with   

Pie chart, Bar chart, Stacked Bar chart, Polar chart, and Radar chart.
