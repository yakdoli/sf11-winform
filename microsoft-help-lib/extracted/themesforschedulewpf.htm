Themes for Schedule WPF

Themes for Schedule WPF provide different skins to make the look and feel attractive.

Four themes are available in Schedule WPF. They are:

·      Blend Theme- Similar to Microsoft Blend style.

·      Office14 Silver Theme – Similar to Microsoft Office 14 Silver style.

·      Office14 Black Theme – Similar to Microsoft Office 14 Black style.

·      Office14 Blue Theme – Similar to Microsoft Office 14 Blue style.

·      VS2010

 

Basic property

 

·      VisualStyle - Sets the themes of the schedule.

 

Applying Blend Theme

Apply Blend theme for Schedule, by using the following code:

 

[XAML]

<schedule:Schedule x:Name="Schedule" VisualStyle="Blend"/>

Figure 9: Blend Theme
