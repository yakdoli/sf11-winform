Through Designer

 

The Splitter control can be created as follows.

 

1.   Drag the Splitter control onto the web application.

2.   To add one or more SplitPanes and optional SplitterBars in-between the SplitPanes, you can directly do through aspx code.

 

The example below demonstrates how to create a Vertical Splitter with four collapsible SplitPanes and SplitterBars with different CollapseModes.

 

[aspx]

 

<cc1:Splitter ID="Splitter1" Layout="Vertical" runat="server" Height="300px" Width="300px" AutoFormat="Office2003 Blue">

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="SplitPane1" runat="server" Height="70px">

        <span>Content of SplitPane1</span>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

    <cc1:SplitterBar ID="SplitterBar1" runat="server" CollapseMode="Forward" />

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="SplitPane2" runat="server" Height="70px">

        <span>Content of SplitPane2</span>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

    <cc1:SplitterBar ID="SplitterBar2" runat="server" CollapseMode="Both" />

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="SplitPane3" runat="server" Height="70px">

        <span>Content of SplitPane3</span>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

    <cc1:SplitterBar ID="SplitterBar3" runat="server" CollapseMode="Backward" />

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="SplitPane4" runat="server" Height="70px">

        <span>Content of SplitPane4</span>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

</cc1:Splitter>

You can define complex structures using several nested Splitters to provide both horizontally and vertically split pages or areas in the page. Following sample demonstrates this.

 

[aspx]

 

<cc1:Splitter ID="Splitter1" Layout="Vertical" runat="server" Height="250px" Width="250px"

    AutoFormat="Office2003 Blue">

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="SplitterContainerPane" runat="server" Height="140px">

        <cc1:Splitter ID="InnerSplitter" runat="server" AutoFormat="Office2003 Blue" BorderWidth="0px">

            <cc1:SplitPane ID="LeftSplitPane" runat="server" Width="120px">

                <span>Left SplitPane</span>

            </cc1:SplitPane>

            <cc1:SplitterBar ID="SplitterBar2" runat="server" CollapseMode="both" />

            <cc1:SplitPane ID="RightSplitPane" runat="server">

                <span>Right SplitPane</span>

            </cc1:SplitPane>

        </cc1:Splitter>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

    <cc1:SplitterBar ID="SplitterBar1" runat="server" CollapseMode="both" />

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="BottomPane" runat="server" Height="110px">

        <span>Bottom SplitPane</span>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

</cc1:Splitter>

To declare a sliding zone you need to drag-and-drop a SlidingZone control into a SplitPane. Then add some SlidingPane controls to the SlidingZone. The following sample demonstrates how to declare left and right sliding zones, containing two sliding panes each.

 

[aspx]

 

<cc1:Splitter ID="Splitter1" Layout="Horizontal" runat="server" Height="300px" Width="300px"

    AutoFormat="Office2003 Blue">

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="LeftZoneContainer" runat="server">

        <cc1:SlidingZone ID="LeftSlidingZone" runat="server" SlideDirection="Right">

            <cc1:SlidingPane ID="LeftPane1" Title="LeftPane1" runat="server" Width="120px"></cc1:SlidingPane>

            <cc1:SlidingPane ID="LeftPane2" Title="LeftPane2" runat="server" Width="120px"></cc1:SlidingPane>

        </cc1:SlidingZone>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

    <cc1:SplitterBar ID="SplitterBar1" runat="server" CollapseMode="both" />

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="MainSplitPane" runat="server">

        <span>Main SplitPane</span>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

    <cc1:SplitterBar ID="SplitterBar2" runat="server" CollapseMode="Both" />

    <cc1:SplitPane ID="RightZoneContainer" runat="server">

        <cc1:SlidingZone ID="RightSlidingZone" runat="server" SlideDirection="Left">

            <cc1:SlidingPane ID="RightPane1" Title="RightPane1" runat="server" Width="120px"></cc1:SlidingPane>

            <cc1:SlidingPane ID="RightPane2" Title="RightPane2" runat="server" Width="120px"></cc1:SlidingPane>

        </cc1:SlidingZone>

    </cc1:SplitPane>

</cc1:Splitter>
