ChartSeriesSkins

 

Chart MVC supports nine built-in themes for the Chart series for the professional representation of charts.

The following are the available skins:

 

·      Pastel

·      Nature

·      GrayScale

·      EarthTone

·      Triad

·      DefaultAlpha

·      Colorful

·      Analog

·      WarmCold
