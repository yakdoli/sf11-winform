Stacking Column Chart

 

Stacking Column Charts are similar to regular column charts except that the Y values stack on top of each other in the specified series order. This helps visualizing the relationship of parts to the whole.

 

[XAML]

 

<syncfusion:ChartSeries Type="StackingColumn" Label="2008" StrokeThickness="2" Interior="#FFEAA700" Name="Series1" DataSource="{StaticResource rainfallData}" BindingPathX="Month" BindingPathsY="Value1" />

<syncfusion:ChartSeries Type="StackingColumn" Label="2009" StrokeThickness="2" Interior="#FFBFD147" Name="Series2" DataSource="{StaticResource rainfallData}" BindingPathX="Month" BindingPathsY="Value2" />

 

 

[C#]

 

series1.ChartType = ChartTypes.StackingColumn;

series2.ChartType = ChartTypes.StackingColumn;
