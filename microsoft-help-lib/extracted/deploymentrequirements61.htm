Deployment Requirements

For using Essential ReportViewer for WPF, you need to deploy the following list of assemblies with your product.

 

·      Syncfusion.Core

·      Syncfusion.Chart.WPF

·      Syncfusion.Compression.Base

·      Syncfusion.DocIO.Base

·      Syncfusion.Gauge.WPF

·      Syncfusion.Grid.WPF

·      Syncfusion.GridCommon.WPF

·      Syncfusion.Linq.Base

·      Syncfusion.Pdf.Base

·      Syncfusion.ReportControls.WPF

·      Syncfusion.ReportViewer.WPF

·      Syncfusion.ReportWriter.Base

·      Syncfusion.Shared.WPF

·      Syncfusion.XlsIO.Base
