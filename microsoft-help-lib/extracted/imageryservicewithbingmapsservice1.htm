Imagery Service with Bing Maps Service

Imagery Service enables Essential Maps to show Bing Maps. Imagery service uses the Bing Maps Imagery Web service to achieve this feature. Bing Maps Imagery Service is the set of APIs that is used to integrate Bing Maps with applications. With zooming and panning features, the maps can be drilled in, drilled out, and navigated. With Bing Maps service, Essential Maps will show aerial views and road views of the world map.
