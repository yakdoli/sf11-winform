Appointments

 

Extensive customization options are provided for creating and displaying the appointments of the Schedule control. Appointments can be resized, dragged, blocked and extended for an entire day (AllDay Appointment).

Appointment timings can be extended or changed, by resizing the appointment or dragging the corners of the appointment. Resizing can be restricted to an individual appointment.

Appointments can also be added and edited at run time.
