Feature Summary

Here is a brief overview of various features of the Circular Gauge.

·      Scales—allows for complex gauges with one or more scales.

·      Frame types—allows you to specify the circular built-in frame style such as full circle, half circle or custom templates.

·      Pointers—complete support for pointers to point the value.

·      State indicator—complete support for state indicator to indicate the value level.

·      Range—full-fledged range support.

·      Image and Labels—customize the appearance of the gauge with meaningful images and labels.

·      Center frame content—customize the appearance of the gauge with Framework Element such as hosting digital gauge on circular gauge.
