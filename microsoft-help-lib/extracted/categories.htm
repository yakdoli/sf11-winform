Categories

        

Resources and appointments can be grouped by Categories.

 

Properties of Categories

 

·      Name: text to be displayed for category of resources in the Schedule

·      BackColor: specifies the back color of the panel of the combined appointments

·      TimeSpanColor: specifies the color of the time span of the combined appointments

·      Expanded: specifies whether to expand the resources on page load

·      Description: gets or sets description for category of resources

·      Info: additional information for category of resources

·      ShortName: gets or sets the text to be displayed in the category of combined appointments

 

Setting Categories By Code

 

[ASPX]

 

<Syncfusion:Schedule ID="Schedule1" runat="server" ViewMode="Horizontal" DayEndHour="20"

DayStartHour="6" TimeInterval="FifteenMin" StartDate="2007-07-25" Height="700"

Width="600" DayNavigatorPosition="HeaderLeft" ResolutionHeight="30" AutoPostBackOnScheduleClick="true"

AutoFormat="Office2007Blue">

<Categories>

<Syncfusion:ScheduleCategory BackColor="CornflowerBlue" Name="Chief" TimeSpanColor="CornflowerBlue" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleCategory BackColor="IndianRed" Name="Subordinate" TimeSpanColor="IndianRed" />

</Categories>

<Resources>

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebResource Name="Mark" Category="Chief" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebResource Category="Chief" Name="Olof" Description="Olof" UniqueID="1" Info="Olof" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebResource Category="Subordinate" Name="Mike" Description="Mike"

UniqueID="2" Info="Mike" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebResource Category="Subordinate" Name="Maria" Description="Maria" UniqueID="3" Info="Maria" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebResource Category="Subordinate" Name="Kenny" Description="Kenny" UniqueID="4" Info="Kenny" />

</Resources>

<Appointments>

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebAppointment Content="Travel" EndTime="07/25/2007 07:00:00" StartTime="07/25/2007 06:30:00" Subject="Travel" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebAppointment EndTime="07/25/2007 07:15:00" Owner="1" StartTime="07/25/2007 06:00:00"

Content="Travel" Subject="Travel" UniqueID="1" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebAppointment Content="Meeting" EndTime="07/25/2007 12:00:00"

Owner="1" StartTime="07/25/2007 09:00:00" Subject="Meeting" UniqueID="2" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebAppointment Content="Dinner" EndTime="07/25/2007 14:00:00"

Owner="1" StartTime="07/25/2007 13:00:00" Subject="Dinner" UniqueID="3" />

<Syncfusion:ScheduleWebAppointment Content="Hard work" EndTime="07/25/2007 19:00:00"

Owner="1" StartTime="07/25/2007 14:00:00" Subject="Hard work" UniqueID="4" />

</Appointments>

<ResourceRowStyle Height="30px" />

<AllDayAppointmentRowStyle Height="30px" />

</Syncfusion:Schedule>
