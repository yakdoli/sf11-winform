Financial Charts

 

These charts are a staple of analytical reports in the financial world. Financial data usually has more than one y value. For example, stock price charts should include high, low, open and close prices for a day. Such data needs to be appropriately rendered in the context of "stock market data". Also, besides actual values, trends in price movement need to be depicted visually.

 

The following charts cater to the above requirements and provide an easy-to-decipher picture of price action.
