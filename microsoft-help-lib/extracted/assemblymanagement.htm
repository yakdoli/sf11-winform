Assembly Management

Assembly Management consists of Assembly Manager and Multi-Target Manager. 

 

The Assembly Manager utility installs Syncfusion Essential Studio assemblies in the GAC and in the Assemblies folders, provided Visual Studio.NET is installed on the development machine.

Note: The Assembly Manager does not require Visual Studio.NET to be installed.
