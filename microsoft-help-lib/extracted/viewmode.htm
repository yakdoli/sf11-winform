View Mode

 

The Schedule control can be displayed in either of the two modes supported: Vertical (Default) and Horizontal.

 

[ASPX]

 

<cc1:Schedule ID="Schedule1" ViewMode ="Horizontal" runat="server">

</syncfusion:Schedule>

 

[C#]

 

this.Schedule1.ViewMode = Syncfusion.Web.UI.WebControls.ScheduleControl.ScheduleViewMode.Horizontal;

 

[VB]

 

Me.Schedule1.ViewMode = Syncfusion.Web.UI.WebControls.ScheduleControl.ScheduleViewMode.Horizontal
