Window Position

The position of the Window can be controlled by the user by setting the property WindowStartUpLocation. The Enum takes the following values:

·      CenterScreen – The window will appear at the center of the Screen.

·      CenterOwner – the window will appear at the center of the Owner Window.

·      Manual - The location of the window can be controlled by Top and Left properties.
